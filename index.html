Garmin SpO2 for OSCAR

Turn the overnight SpO2 and pulse recorded by your Garmin watch into files OSCAR can import, so you can see them next to your CPAP data.

    Import into OSCAR

    1. Unzip the downloaded file.
    2. In OSCAR, choose Import Viatom/Wellue data.
    3. Open the oscar_viatom folder, select every file (Cmd+A or Ctrl+A) and confirm.
    1. Unzip the downloaded file.
    2. In OSCAR, open the Oximetry Wizard and choose to import from a data file.
    3. Pick one .dat file from the md300 folder and choose “use the oximeter’s internal clock”. Repeat for each night.
    4. If you abandon an import halfway, restart OSCAR before the next one.

    Sleep stages: in OSCAR, choose Import Dreem data and pick garmin_sleep_stages.csv in the oscar_dreem folder. Nights already imported are skipped, and OSCAR shows a sleep-stage graph.

    Breathing disruptions and restless moments: in OSCAR, choose Import Somnopose data and pick garmin_somnopose.csv in the oscar_somnopose folder. Breathing disruptions appear in the Orientation graph (0 not measured, 10 none, 20 low, 30 medium, 40 high), restless moments in the Inclination graph.

    Journal: OSCAR’s Journal import relies on the note box shown on screen, so follow these steps exactly. 1) File → Backup Journal. 2) Display a day that is not in garmin_journal.xml (for example before your first night with the watch) and whose Notes box is not empty: type a temporary “x” if needed. 3) File → Import Journal and pick garmin_journal.xml in the oscar_journal folder. 4) Remove the “x”, change day and check a couple of days. If the note box is empty during the import, OSCAR erases the notes of the imported days; if the day on screen is in the file, its note is replaced by the Garmin one.

    Everything runs in your browser: your files are never uploaded anywhere. Times use this computer’s time zone ().

    Breathing disruptions are Garmin’s own estimate from the watch’s SpO2 and breathing data. They are only measured while Pulse Ox is recording, and they are not apneas as counted by a CPAP: compare their trend with your AHI, not minute by minute.

    Sleep movement isn’t stored in .fit files. It’s added when you also drop Garmin sleep .json files that contain it, such as the sleep.json files saved by the garmin2oscar Python script; it then appears as OSCAR’s Movement graph (Viatom format only, value = Garmin activity level × 10).

    On a night that starts on a daylight-saving-change day, readings before midnight are left out, otherwise OSCAR labels that night’s time axis one hour off.

    A wrist sensor is not a medical oximeter: one reading per minute, and flat plateaus often mean poor contact. Talk to your doctor about your results.
